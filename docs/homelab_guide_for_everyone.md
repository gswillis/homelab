# Home system guide for everyone

Nov 24, 2026 · @Sir

This is a small computer in our house that blocks ads, keeps our photos, and runs our smart sensors. You do not need to know anything technical to use it.

## What is it?

There is one small computer in our home. We call it the box. It runs quietly all day and does three jobs for us. It sits next to the internet router and connects to it with a cable.

Everything stays inside our home. Nothing here can be reached from outside, so strangers cannot get in.

## What the box does

| Job | In plain words | What you will notice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ad blocking | Stops many ads and trackers before they reach your phone, TV or computer | Fewer ads on websites and apps |
| Photo storage | Keeps our family photos and videos on a hard drive in our house instead of on Google | Your phone saves photos to our home drive |
| Smart sensors | Lets our door, window and motion sensors talk to one place | Sensors work together, even if the internet is down |

## What is inside and around the box

The box is not alone. A few other parts help it work. You do not need to touch any of them.

| Part | What it is | Why we have it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The box | A small, quiet desktop computer | It runs everything in this guide |
| Fast drive | A small, fast storage chip inside the box | It holds the programs that run the system |
| Photo drive | A big hard drive that can hold about four times our photos today | It keeps our photos and videos |
| Backup drive | A second hard drive, plugged in only now and then | It holds a spare copy of our photos, in case the photo drive breaks |
| Online copy | A third copy kept on the internet, scrambled so only we can read it | It protects our photos from fire or theft. We may add this later |
| Battery backup | A big battery the box plugs into | It keeps the box on for a short time if the power goes out |
| Two small radio sticks | Small plugs in the side of the box | They let the box talk to our sensors, which do not use Wi-Fi |
| Network cable | A cable from the box to the router | It connects the box to our home internet |

## The programs on the box

Programs are the jobs the box does. Each job has a name. You might hear these names, so here is what they mean.

| Name | What it does, in plain words |
| --- | --- |
| Proxmox | The base program. It keeps each job in its own space, so a problem in one job does not stop the others |
| Pi-hole | Blocks many ads. It also helps our devices find websites, so when the box is off, the internet seems broken |
| Home Assistant | The place where all our sensors connect and work together |
| Immich | Our photo program. It keeps your phone photos and lets you search them |
| Forgejo | Keeps our settings and notes safe, with a history, like an undo button for the system |

The system is kept up to date by the person in charge, about once a month. You do not need to do anything.

## Where the settings and notes are kept

The box needs instructions to do its jobs. These are small text files. They say things like which sensors we have and what should happen when a door opens.

We keep these files in a safe folder that remembers every change. It works like an undo button for the whole system. If someone makes a mistake, the person in charge can go back to how things were before.

- The main copy is kept on the box, in the program called Forgejo.
- A second private copy is kept in a private online account that only the person in charge can open, so it is safe if the box breaks.
- Passwords and secret codes are never kept in this folder.
- You do not need to open or change any of it. Ask the person in charge if you are curious.

## Photos and videos

There is a photo app on your phone. It copies your new photos and videos to the hard drive in our house.

- **It only copies when you are at home on our Wi-Fi.** When you are out, the photos wait on your phone. They copy over when you walk back in.
- **Your photos stay in our house.** They are not stored on Google.
- **You can search your photos.** Try typing a person's name or something in the picture, like a dog or a beach.
- **Ask before deleting lots of photos at once.** It is easy to remove something by mistake.

## The box and the little battery

The box is plugged into a battery backup. It keeps the box running for a short time if the power goes out, so nothing gets damaged.

- Please leave the box plugged in and switched on.
- Please do not unplug it to charge your phone or use the socket.
- Please keep dust and drinks away from it.

## If something stops working

| What you see | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| Wi-Fi shows connected, but no websites load on any device | Check that the box is switched on and plugged in. If it is off, tell the person in charge before turning it back on |
| The internet is slow or odd after a power cut | Wait five minutes. The box needs a little time to start again |
| One website or app will not load, but others work | Ad blocking can sometimes block a page by mistake. Tell the person in charge the name of the site |
| Photos are not copying from your phone | Check that you are on our home Wi-Fi, then open the photo app and wait a minute |
| A sensor seems wrong or dead | Tell the person in charge. It may just need a new battery |

## Please do not

- Do not unplug the box, the router, or the battery backup.
- Do not press buttons on the box or hold the power button down.
- Do not plug other things into the box.
- Do not share our Wi-Fi password with visitors. Ask for the guest Wi-Fi instead.
